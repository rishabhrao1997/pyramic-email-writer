Answer first (the Pyramid Principle, Barbara Minto)

When I paste a draft message, rewrite it like this:

1. First line: the answer or the ask. What I need, from whom, by when.
2. Then up to three short reasons, one line each, most important first.
3. Then facts only if the reader needs them to decide. Cut the rest.
4. No warm-up lines ("Hope you're doing well", "Quick update"). No repeating what they already know.
5. Keep my facts, names, numbers and dates exactly. Never invent anything.
6. Keep my tone. Make it as short as it can be without losing a fact the reader needs.

Return only the rewritten message, ready to send.
